# MCPS ELD instructional programs

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/instruction/
Retrieved for this summary: October 22, 2026. This is a paraphrased retrieval aid; follow the linked MCPS page for updates.

## WIDA standards and proficiency

MCPS describes five WIDA English language development standards: social and instructional language, language arts, mathematics, science, and social studies. Its page describes six proficiency stages, from Entering through Reaching. Teachers should provide content-access supports at every proficiency stage. The page states an overall level of 5.0 as an ESOL exit point; use current Maryland criteria and individual assessment records for any actual exit decision.

## School support and programs

Instructional specialists support schools and professional learning. The page links to elementary, middle, and high school instruction and describes METS for students with limited or interrupted schooling, SEPA as a career pathway for some Spanish-speaking high school students, and CREA for older learners whose interrupted schooling affects graduation timing.

## Elementary instruction

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/es/
Pre-K uses language-rich, multimodal approaches. In kindergarten through grade 5, ELD instruction and classroom supports develop academic English while maintaining access to grade-level content. MCPS describes co-teaching, plug-in, and pull-out service models. Grades 3–5 students with a SLIFE designation attend their home school and may receive support through a SLIFE coach.

## Middle school instruction

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/ms/
Middle school ELD courses align with grades 6–8 English content and develop listening, speaking, reading, and writing. MCPS describes sheltered, co-taught, single-period, and double-period arrangements based on proficiency and grade.

## High school instruction

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/high/
High school ELD instruction aligns with grade-level English content. The current program page says students may receive up to two English credits from ELD courses over four semesters. It describes grade-level English plus ELD seminar, co-teaching, or a dually certified ELD teacher for grades 11–12 and for students at English proficiency levels 4.0–4.4. This current page differs from the older IOD-RA credit description; confirm credit decisions with current MCPS guidance.
