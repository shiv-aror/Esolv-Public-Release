# MCPS elementary ELD instruction

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/es/
Reviewed: October 22, 2026. Paraphrased retrieval aid; consult MCPS for current decisions.

## Shared instruction and access

MCPS describes elementary ELD as a shared responsibility of ELD teachers, classroom teachers, other staff, and Emergent Multilingual Learners. Kindergarten through grade 5 students receiving ELD services receive direct language instruction and support for access to grade-level content. Classroom teachers provide academic-English supports in content instruction.

## Prekindergarten

The page describes language-rich settings where children may develop English and a home language together. Staff use multiple modalities and the WIDA Early Years framework.

## Grades 3–5 students with limited or interrupted formal education

Students designated SLIFE attend their home school with same-age peers. A SLIFE coach may support school staff through professional learning, modeling, co-teaching, planning, and collaboration with case-management teams.

## Service models

MCPS describes co-teaching by ELD and general education teachers, plug-in instruction within the general education classroom, and pull-out instruction. These models integrate language development with content and can support reading, listening, writing, and speaking.
