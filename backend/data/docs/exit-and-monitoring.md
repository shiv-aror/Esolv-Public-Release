# Reclassification, Exit & Monitoring

> SAMPLE CONTENT for development. The exact MCPS and state thresholds must come from the official documents.

## Exit (reclassification)
A student exits EL status when they demonstrate English proficiency under the state's exit criteria. The state criteria are based on the ACCESS for ELLs overall composite score and may include additional measures. [Insert exact current state and MCPS thresholds here. Do not guess a number.]

An exit decision is made by a team and must be documented. Teachers should not tell families a student has exited until the team has finalized the decision.

## Monitoring
Federal law requires schools to monitor the academic progress of former ELs for **at least four years** after they exit. Monitoring includes:
- reviewing grades and state assessment results each year
- watching for academic struggles that could relate to language
- re-entering ELD services if the student is not succeeding and language is a factor, with family notification

## Documentation
Record the exit date, the criteria used, who made the decision, and the monitoring schedule. Keep these in the student's record.
