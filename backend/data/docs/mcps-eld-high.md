# MCPS high school ELD instruction

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/high/
Reviewed: October 22, 2026. Paraphrased retrieval aid; consult MCPS for current credits and scheduling.

## Instruction

High school ELD courses align with grade-level English Language Arts content and develop listening, speaking, reading, writing, academic discourse, and analysis of complex texts. The page emphasizes collaboration among educators and the value of students' home language and culture.

## Credits and pathways

The current page says EMLs may earn up to two English credits from ELD courses across up to four semesters. Grades 9–10 have EML English and ELD Seminar course options, including double-period and single-period arrangements. Students at English proficiency levels 4.0–4.4 and students in grades 11–12 enroll in grade-level English and receive ELD services through an ELD Seminar, a co-taught class, or a dually certified ELD teacher. This current credit description differs from the older 2012 IOD-RA text; verify actual credit decisions with current MCPS guidance.
