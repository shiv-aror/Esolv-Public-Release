# ELD Paperwork & Compliance Timeline

> SAMPLE CONTENT for development. Replace with the official MCPS calendar and forms. Federal timelines below are general; confirm the local version.

## At enrollment
1. **Home Language Survey (HLS)**: completed by the family at enrollment. If a language other than English is indicated, the student is screened.
2. **English language proficiency screener**: administered when the HLS indicates a language other than English. Under federal rules, students enrolling at the start of the school year are identified within **30 days** of enrollment, and students who enroll later in the year are identified within **2 weeks**.
3. **Parent notification of placement**: families must be notified in writing, in a language they understand where practicable, of the student's identification as an English learner and the program placement. Federal timelines mirror the identification timelines: 30 days at the start of the year, 2 weeks later in the year.

## During the school year
- **ELD plan**: document the student's proficiency level, program model, services, and accommodations. Update after new ACCESS scores arrive.
- **Progress reporting**: share progress toward English proficiency with families, ideally at report card times.
- **Accommodation review**: review at least annually. See the Accommodations Guide.

## Spring
- **ACCESS for ELLs** testing during the state window.
- Verify the testing accommodations listed in each student's plan before the window opens.

## End of year
- **Annual exit review**: use ACCESS results and other criteria to determine whether any students are ready for reclassification. See Reclassification, Exit & Monitoring.
- Update records and prepare the next year's service lists.
