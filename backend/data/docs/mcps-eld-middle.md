# MCPS middle school ELD instruction

Source: https://www.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/curriculum/eld/ms/
Reviewed: October 22, 2026. Paraphrased retrieval aid; consult MCPS for current scheduling and course codes.

## Instruction

Middle school ELD courses align with grade-level English Language Arts standards in grades 6–8. They develop academic English through listening, speaking, reading, and writing, including grammar and formal and informal language. MCPS describes instruction that builds oral language and supports reading and writing.

## Scheduling and courses

The page describes sheltered, co-taught, single-period, and double-period English classes selected to meet students' academic language proficiency. It associates these pathways with WIDA ACCESS English Language Proficiency levels 1.0–4.4. Course sequences for grades 6, 7, and 8 each have EML I and II double-period options and an EML III single-period option. Verify the current course catalog and individual placement before scheduling.
