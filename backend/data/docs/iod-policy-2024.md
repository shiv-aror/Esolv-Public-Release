# MCPS Policy IOD: English Language Development Programs and Services

Source: https://ww2.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/departments/policy/detail.aspx?policyID=IOD&recID=234&sectionID=9
Board amendment: June 11, 2024. This is a paraphrased retrieval aid; consult the linked policy for official wording.

## Purpose and responsibility

Policy IOD uses an asset-based approach to Emergent Multilingual Learners (EMLs). Their academic success is a shared responsibility among students, families, communities, and all educators. Eligible students receive English Language Development (ELD) instruction in listening, speaking, reading, and writing while retaining access to challenging grade-level content. Their home languages and cultures are educational assets.

## Identification and reclassification

Qualified staff administer a federally required English language proficiency assessment when two of the three Maryland Home Language Survey answers identify a language other than English at initial MCPS enrollment. Program exit and reclassification follow federal and Maryland State Department of Education criteria. A school's ELD team monitors students who exit and provides appropriate support for two years.

## Instruction, assessment, and accommodations

Schools provide services responsive to eligible students' needs. Assessments must be administered validly and reasonably, with accommodations to which students are entitled. Staff receive professional learning on instructional and assessment accommodations. When a disability is suspected, evaluation tools in the student's dominant language are used when available; other evidence is considered when such a tool is unavailable.

## Families and reporting

MCPS calls for meaningful two-way family communication about learning, educational benchmarks, course sequences, and opportunities. Academic progress of current and former ELD participants is reported to the Board annually, and MCPS fulfills federal and state reporting requirements.
