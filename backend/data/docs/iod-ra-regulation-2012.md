# MCPS Regulation IOD-RA: English for Speakers of Other Languages

Source: https://ww2.montgomeryschoolsmd.org/departments/policy/detail.aspx?policyID=IOD-RA&recID=235&sectionID=9
Last revised: August 22, 2012. This is a paraphrased retrieval aid. Some terminology and exit language predates Policy IOD's 2024 amendment and current state guidance; verify current decisions with official sources.

## Identification and placement

Students referred from the Residency and International Admissions office or a school take the state-mandated English language proficiency placement test before ESOL enrollment. For eligible students, the ESOL teacher arranges placement using instructional pathways and sends written parent notification under applicable guidelines.

## Grading and progress

Students are evaluated in ESOL and other classes under Policy IKA and Regulation IKA-RA. For Level 1 or 2 students, an NEP grade may be used in a non-ESOL course for no more than two consecutive quarters when more time is needed to assess content mastery. Students at risk of failing or dropping more than one letter grade should receive an Interim Progress Report under Policy IKA.

## Documented accommodations

Identified accommodations in a student's English language learner documentation should be provided during non-ESOL instruction and content assessments. The regulation describes final exam options for high school ESOL 1–3 and regular final exams with identified accommodations for ESOL 4–5. These rules must be read with current assessment guidance and each student's actual plan; no sample record establishes an entitlement.

## Credit, completion, and monitoring

The regulation describes credit for ESOL Levels 1–5, including a half English credit per semester and possible dual enrollment with English. Its 2012 exit criterion refers to Annual Measurable Achievement Objective II; current Policy IOD instead refers to federal and MSDE reclassification criteria. After exit, the regulation describes a two-year R-ELL support plan and counselor coordination.
