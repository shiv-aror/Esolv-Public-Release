## Overview
ACCESS for ELLs is the annual summative assessment of English language proficiency for students identified as English learners. It is developed by WIDA. It measures four language domains: **Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing**.

## Scores
- Each domain receives a proficiency level score from 1.0 to 6.0.
- An **Overall composite** score weights the domains. Literacy (Reading and Writing) and Oral Language (Listening and Speaking) composites are also reported.
- The six WIDA proficiency levels are: 1 Entering, 2 Emerging, 3 Developing, 4 Expanding, 5 Bridging, 6 Reaching.

## Who tests
All students identified as ELs take ACCESS every year until they meet exit criteria. Students with significant cognitive disabilities who qualify may take Alternate ACCESS. The IEP team determines eligibility for the alternate.

## Accommodations on ACCESS
Some ACCESS supports are available to all students (for example, extended time within the test day and breaks). Others are accommodations for students with documented needs, and these must be recorded in advance in the student's plan. Only use accommodations that are allowed by the current WIDA Accessibility and Accommodations Supplement.

## After testing
Score reports arrive in spring. Teachers use domain scores to plan instruction. Families receive a score report and a summary. Scores are also used in accountability calculations (see the Accountability Overview).
